# athos-sandbox

Slack sandbox, nothing is sent to Slack

Mention @athos to ask the bot, as in Slack. Messages without a mention are posted to the thread but get no reply, and the bot reads them when you ask it to take a note. Change "You are" to act as another user, e.g. one who is not allowed to use the bot. Restarting the sandbox starts an empty channel.
